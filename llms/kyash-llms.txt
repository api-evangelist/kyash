# Kyash

> Kyash Inc. (株式会社Kyash) is a Tokyo-based fintech that operates a consumer mobile wallet and Visa prepaid card in Japan, and sells a B2B payout service to businesses. Kyash is a registered Funds Transfer Service Provider (Kanto Local Finance Bureau No. 00082) and third-party Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuer (No. 00698). Its API surface is sales-gated: there is no public developer portal, no published OpenAPI, and no public API reference.

## Company

- [Kyash](https://kyash.co/): Consumer wallet, Visa prepaid card, money transfer, and personal finance management app for the Japanese market.
- [Company profile](https://kyash.co/company): Founded 2015-01-23. HQ Aoyama, Minato-ku, Tokyo. CEO Shinichi Takatori. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified, PCI DSS certified, TRUSTe certified.

## APIs

- [Kyash Payout (Kyash 送金サービス)](https://kyash.co/biz/payout): B2B mass-payout service. Sends funds to Japanese domestic bank accounts or to Kyash app wallets at a flat ¥50 per transfer, 24/7. Two integration modes: bulk file upload (Zengin full-bank format or CSV, ~2 weeks to launch) and direct API integration (~1 month to launch), including a balance-checking API. Access is granted through a sales inquiry; no public documentation, base URL, authentication reference, or specification is published.
- [Payout inquiry](https://kyash.co/biz/payout/inquiry): Request access to the Payout service and its API.
- [Payout terms of service](https://kyash.co/legal/terms/payout)

Historical: "Kyash Direct", announced 2019-04-25 and launched 2019-10-04, was Kyash's API-based Visa card issuer processing platform sold to other businesses. It no longer has a public product page or developer documentation on kyash.co.
